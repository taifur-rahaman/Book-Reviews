## 📖 About

Stephen Chbosky is an American novelist, screenwriter, and film director. He is best known for writing the coming-of-age novel *[The Perks of Being a Wallflower](../titles/the_perks_of_being_a_wallflower.md)* (1999), as well as for screenwriting and directing the film adaptation of the same book.

---

## ✍️ Writing Style

- **Epistolary Format**: Known for using letters or diary entries to tell intimate, first-person stories.
- **Emotional Authenticity**: Captures the raw and often painful experiences of adolescence with honesty and empathy.
- **Character-Driven**: Focuses deeply on the internal lives and relationships of his characters.
